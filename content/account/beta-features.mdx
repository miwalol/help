**Beta features are new tools or options that are still being tested.** They usually work, but might have small bugs since they're not fully finished yet. The goal is to gather your feedback so we can fix issues and polish them before releasing them to everyone.

To find them, go to your [Dashboard > Settings > Beta Features](https://miwa.lol/dashboard/settings/beta-features).

## Joining a beta

Each available beta is listed with a short description and a switch. Flip the switch to opt in, and flip it back whenever you want to leave.

<Aside type="info">
  The list is often empty. We only put a feature there while it's actively being tested, so if you see *"No beta features available yet"*, there's simply nothing in testing right now - check back later.
</Aside>

## Sending feedback

Once you're enrolled, you'll see a thumbs up and a thumbs down next to the feature. Use them to tell us whether it works for you.

Both open a short form where you can explain what you liked or what went wrong, and optionally attach a screenshot. This feedback is the whole point of the programme, so please do send it, even if it's just "works fine".

<Aside type="tip">
  Found a real bug? Reporting it also counts toward the [Bug Hunter badge](/how-to/bug-hunter-badge/).
</Aside>
