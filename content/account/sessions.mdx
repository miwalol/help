**A session is created every time you log in to Miwa.lol**, whether from a browser or from our mobile app. The sessions page lets you see them all and log out the ones you don't recognize.

To find it, go to your [Dashboard > Settings > Sessions](https://miwa.lol/dashboard/settings/sessions).

## What you'll see

Sessions are split into two groups:

- **Current session** - the device you're reading this from.
- **Other sessions** - everywhere else you're still logged in.

Each entry shows:

- The **browser**, or *iOS app* / *Android app* if you signed in from our mobile app.
- The **operating system**, or the device model for mobile app sessions.
- The **approximate location** it was last used from (city, region, and country).
- **When it was last active.**

<Aside type="info">
  **Sessions expire 7 days after you log in.** Once one expires you'll be asked to log in again on that device, and it disappears from this list - so everything you see here is a session that's still valid.
</Aside>

## Revoking a session

To log out one device, click the delete icon on that session. To log out everywhere except the device you're using, click **Log out all other devices**.

<Aside type="warning" title="See something you don't recognize?">
  If a session appears that isn't yours, log it out, then [change your password](/account/security/#changing-your-password) right away and consider enabling [two-factor authentication](/account/security/#two-factor-authentication-2fa).
</Aside>

<Aside type="info">
  Locations are approximate. They're derived from the IP address the session was last used from, so a VPN, a mobile network, or your ISP's routing can place you in a different city or region than you actually are. Sessions using the Tor network are shown as *Tor*.
</Aside>
