**The analytics page shows how your profile is doing:** how many people visited it, where they came from, and which of your links they clicked.

To find it, go to your [Dashboard > Analytics](https://miwa.lol/dashboard/analytics).

## Choosing a period

Everything on the page is scoped to the period you pick in the selector at the top right.

| Period    | Available to        |
|-----------|---------------------|
| 1 day     | Everyone            |
| 3 days    | Everyone            |
| 7 days    | Everyone            |
| 14 days   | Premium             |
| 30 days   | Premium             |
| 90 days   | Premium             |
| 1 year    | Premium             |

<Aside type="info">
  Free accounts can look back **7 days**, Premium accounts up to **1 year**. Your data isn't deleted when you're on a free account, so upgrading later reveals the longer periods.
</Aside>

## Statistics

Four counters sit at the top of the page. Each one shows a badge comparing it to the **previous period of the same length**, so on a 7-day view you're comparing this week to the week before.

### Link clicks

How many times your links were clicked.

### Views

How many times your profile page was loaded.

### Unique views

How many distinct visitors you had, counted by IP address. This is always lower than *Views*, since one person refreshing your page counts once here but several times there.

### Likes

How many likes you received during the period.

## Views statistics

A graph of your views across the period. The grouping adapts to the range you picked:

- **1 day** - by hour.
- **3 to 30 days** - by day.
- **90 days** - by week.
- **1 year** - by month.

## Top referrers

Where your visitors came from: the site that linked them to you. Visits with no referrer - someone typing your URL, opening a bookmark, or following a link from an app that strips it - are grouped under **Direct/None**.

Well-known platforms are grouped under a single friendly name, so a visit through X's `t.co` shortener or the Twitter app is simply counted as *X*.

## Most clicked links

Your links, ranked by how many clicks they got in the period.

## Top countries

The countries your visitors came from, based on their IP address. Visitors using the Tor network are listed separately, since their real country can't be determined.

<Aside type="info">
  Country data is approximate: a visitor on a VPN or a proxy is counted in whatever country it exits from.
</Aside>

## Devices

A breakdown of visits by device type: **Desktop**, **Mobile**, and **Tablet**.

## Liked by <Badge text="Premium feature" />

The list of users who liked your profile during the period, most recent first. Free accounts see the total count but not who's behind it.

<Aside type="tip">
  Seeing *"No data available yet"*? That just means nobody visited during the period you selected. Try a longer period, or share your profile to get things moving.
</Aside>
