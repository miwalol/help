**Connections link an external account to your Miwa.lol account.** They let you log in with that account instead of your password, and in Discord's case they unlock a few extra features.

To manage them, go to your [Dashboard > Settings > Connections](https://miwa.lol/dashboard/settings/connections).

## Available connections

### Discord

Linking Discord lets you:

- Show your [Discord presence](/cards/examples/#discord-presence) on your profile.
- Use your [Discord avatar](/customize/assets/#use-discord-avatar) as your profile avatar.
- Log in with Discord.
- Get special roles on our [Discord server](https://discord.gg/miwa).

### Google

Linking Google lets you log in with your Google account.

### Apple

Linking Apple lets you log in with your Apple account.

## Linking and unlinking

Each connection has a **Link** button that sends you to that platform to authorize Miwa.lol. Once you come back, the connection shows the account it's linked to.

To remove one, click **Unlink**. We'll send you a confirmation email whenever a connection is unlinked, so you'll know if someone else does it.

<Aside type="tip">
  If you signed up with Discord, Google, or Apple, you may never have set a password. Unlinking is still safe: your account always has an email address, so you can use *Forgot password?* on the [login page](https://miwa.lol/login) to set one and get back in.
</Aside>
