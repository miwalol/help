**Miwa.lol has a leaderboard, accessible at https://miwa.lol/leaderboard, that ranks users based on their views and likes.**
By default, the leaderboard is sorted by likes (most likes first), but you can also sort it by views. Clicking a column again flips the order, so you can look at it from either end.

## Finding your position

Currently, the only way to find your position is to open the leaderboard page and browse through it page by page until you find yourself. We know this isn't ideal, and we're working on a better solution!

## I'm not in the leaderboard, why?

Ranking is computed live from your current likes and views, so a new like or view is reflected the next time the page is loaded.

There are two reasons you might not appear:

- **You have no likes or views yet.** Share your profile and they'll come.
- **You hid your views or your likes.** See below.

## I don't want to be in the leaderboard <Badge text="Requires Premium" />

**Hiding your views or your likes in [Dashboard > Customize](https://miwa.lol/dashboard/customize), under the *Privacy* tab, removes you from the leaderboard.** You'll still see your own views and likes on your dashboard and profile.

<Aside type="info">
  Hiding **either** one is enough: since the leaderboard ranks on both, a profile that hides one of them is left out entirely.
</Aside>

<Aside type="tip">
  If you've hidden one of them, a notice appears at the top of the leaderboard page reminding you why you aren't listed, with a link back to your customization settings.
</Aside>
