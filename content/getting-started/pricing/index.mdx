**Miwa.lol Premium is available as a lifetime payment of €5.99.** This means that you pay once and get access to all the Premium features forever.

## Purchasing

To purchase Miwa.lol Premium, go to https://miwa.lol/pricing and click on *Purchase Premium*. You will be redirected to a secure payment page where you can enter your payment details.

### Accepted Payment Methods

When you check out, you choose between paying **by card** or **by crypto**. The card option opens a secure payment page that also accepts:

- Credit/Debit Cards
- PayPal
- Apple Pay (requires using Safari)
- Google Pay (requires using Google Chrome)
- Revolut Pay

We also accept the majority of third-party providers such as iDEAL, Bancontact, etc.

### Gifting

**You can buy Premium for someone else.** On the [pricing page](https://miwa.lol/pricing), click the <Gift /> button next to *Purchase Premium*, search for the person by their username, optionally write them a message, and pay as usual. They'll be notified once the payment goes through.

The verified badge can be gifted the same way, from its own card.

## Free vs Premium

**The full feature comparison lives on the pricing page: https://miwa.lol/pricing#compare.** It's the source of truth and is always up to date, so we won't repeat all of it here.

These are the limits people ask about most often:

| Feature           | Free   | Premium   |
|-------------------|--------|-----------|
| Social links      | 5      | Unlimited |
| Cards             | 2      | 6         |
| Card rows         | 1      | Up to 3   |
| Audio tracks      | 2      | 8         |
| Analytics history | 7 days | 1 year    |

## Refund Policy

As explained in our [Terms of Service](https://miwa.lol/terms), we **do not offer refunds for Miwa.lol Premium**. Once you purchase Premium, you will have access to all the features for life.
We can only offer refunds in the case of a double charge or an unauthorized purchase.
