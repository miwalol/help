## Collapsing the sidebar

On larger screens, the <PanelLeftClose /> button next to the Miwa.lol logo collapses the sidebar down to just its icons, giving the page more room. Click it again to expand it back.

<Aside type="info">
  While collapsed, hovering an icon shows the name of the section it leads to.
</Aside>

On small screens the sidebar is a drawer instead: open it with the menu button, and close it with the ✕ or by tapping outside it.

## Settings tabs

The <Settings /> **Settings** section is itself split into tabs:

* **General** - your username, display name, alias, custom domain, email address, and the language of the dashboard.
* **Beta Features** - features still in testing you can opt into. [Learn more](/account/beta-features/)
* **Security** - password, two-factor authentication, passkeys, and your API key. [Learn more](/account/security/)
* **Sessions** - everywhere you're logged in. [Learn more](/account/sessions/)
* **Connections** - your linked Discord, Google, and Apple accounts. [Learn more](/account/connections/)
