You can adjust some privacy settings for your page, such as hiding views, likes, and join date, as well as controlling search engine indexing.

To find them, go to your [Dashboard > Customize](https://miwa.lol/dashboard/customize), then go to the *Privacy* tab.

<Aside type="tip">
  Enabling or disabling these options affects both your profile page and the API.
</Aside>

## Options

### Hide views

When enabled, the view counter will be hidden on your page, as well as on the leaderboard.

### Hide likes

When enabled, the like button and like count will be hidden on your page, as well as on the leaderboard.

<Aside type="warning">
  Hiding your views **or** your likes removes you from the [leaderboard](/getting-started/leaderboard/) entirely, since it ranks profiles on both.
</Aside>

### Hide join date

When enabled, the date you joined Miwa.lol will be hidden on your page.

### Search engine indexing

When enabled, your page will be indexed by search engines such as Google, Bing, and DuckDuckGo. This will make it easier for people to find your page through search engines.

<Aside type="info">
  It may take some time for search engines to update their indexes after you enable this option, and unfortunately, we cannot speed up this process. Updates can take anywhere from a few days to a few weeks.
</Aside>

## Hiding your badges

Badge visibility isn't part of this tab. To hide your badges, go to the [Badges](https://miwa.lol/dashboard/badges) page, where you can either hide all of them at once or toggle each badge individually.

Read more about it on the [Badges](/account/badges/#hiding-and-reordering-your-badges) page.
