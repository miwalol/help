**Your profile card is the panel that holds your avatar, username, description, badges and links.** Everything on this page is in the *General* tab of your [Dashboard > Customize](https://miwa.lol/dashboard/customize).

## Description

The text shown under your username. It supports **Markdown**, so you can add bold, italics, links, and more.

### Typewriter description <Badge text="Premium feature" />

Instead of a static description, you can have your card type out several texts one after another. Click **Manage Typewriter settings** above the description field to open the settings.

- **Texts:** up to 10 texts, cycled through in order.
- **Speed:** how long each character takes to appear, from 10 to 300 ms.
- **Delete speed:** how fast a text is erased before the next one, from 10 to 300 ms.

<Aside type="info">
  While the typewriter is enabled, the normal description field is disabled - the typewriter texts replace it.
</Aside>

## Appearance

### Profile opacity

How opaque the card is, from 0 to 100%. Lower values let more of your background show through.

### Profile blur

How much the area behind the card is blurred, from 0 to 100px. This gives the frosted-glass look.

## Profile details

### Location

A free-text location shown on your card, up to 50 characters. It's just text, so you can write anything you like.

### Pronouns

Your pronouns, up to 30 characters. They appear beneath your username.

### Join date format <Badge text="Premium feature" />

How the date you joined Miwa.lol is displayed:

- **Relative:** for example, *2 months ago*.
- **Full date:** for example, *January 14, 2026*.
- **Month & year:** for example, *January 2026*.
- **Year only:** for example, *2026*.

<Aside type="tip">
  Prefer not to show it at all? You can hide your join date entirely in the [Privacy](/customize/privacy/#hide-join-date) tab.
</Aside>

### Remove branding <Badge text="Premium feature" />

Removes the *Claim profile* banner, which is only shown to visitors who aren't logged in.

## Animations

### Tilt card <Badge text="Premium feature" />

Makes your profile card tilt when a visitor hovers over it.

### Beat shake <Badge text="Premium feature" />

Makes your profile card pulse to the beat of your audio.

- **Intensity:** how far the card moves on each beat.
- **Sensitivity:** how easily a beat sets it off. Raise it for quieter music.

<Aside type="info">
  Only visitors to your page see the effect. The preview in the dashboard stays still, and so does the card for anyone whose device is set to reduce motion.
</Aside>

## Border <Badge text="Premium feature" />

Draws a border around your profile card.

### Border roundness

How rounded the card's corners are: **None**, **Small**, **Medium**, or **Large**. This applies whether or not the border itself is shown.

### Show border

Turns the border on. Once enabled, you can set:

- **Border style:**
  - **Static** - a plain, solid border.
  - **Dashed** - a dashed border.
  - **Shimmer** - a highlight that sweeps around the border.
  - **Pulse** - the border fades in and out.
- **Border color**
- **Border width**, from 1 to 8px.
- **Border opacity**, from 0 to 100%.

<Aside type="info">
  The animated styles (*Shimmer* and *Pulse*) stay still for visitors whose device is set to reduce motion.
</Aside>
