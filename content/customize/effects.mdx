**Effects are the finishing touches on your page:** a glow on your username, particles trailing your visitors' cursor, an animation when the page opens, and more.

To find them, go to your [Dashboard > Customize](https://miwa.lol/dashboard/customize), then go to the *Colors & Effects* tab and scroll down to the *Effects* section.

## Glow effect

Adds a soft glow around parts of your profile. You can enable it independently on:

- **Username**
- **Links**
- **Badges**

The glow takes its color from the element it's applied to: your [badge color](/customize/colors/#badges-color) for badges, and each platform's brand color for links - or your [links color](/customize/colors/#links-color) if *Monochrome links* is enabled.

## Cursor effect <Badge text="Premium feature" />

Adds an animation that follows your visitors' cursor:

- **None**
- **Fairy dust** - a trail of sparkles. It uses your [accent color](/customize/colors/#accent-color).
- **Following dot** - a dot that trails slightly behind the cursor.
- **Ghost cursor** - fading copies of the cursor left behind as it moves.
- **Oneko** - a cat that chases the cursor around the page.

<Aside type="tip">
  This is different from a [custom cursor](/customize/assets/#custom-cursor), which replaces the cursor image itself. You can use both at once.
</Aside>

## Background effect

Applies a filter or an animation over your background:

- **None**
- **Blur**
- **Grayscale**
- **Sepia**
- **Old TV**
- **Snowflakes** - falling snowflakes over your background.

## Opening animation <Badge text="Premium feature" />

How your profile card appears when someone opens your page:

- **None**
- **Fade**
- **Pop**
- **Slide up**
- **Slide down**
- **Zoom**
- **Blur in**

## Username effect

Applies an animated style to your username. Click **Choose a username effect** to pick one.

<Aside type="info">
  Unlike the other options on this tab, the username effect is saved as soon as you pick it - you don't need to press *Save*.
</Aside>
