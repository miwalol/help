You can customize how your page tab appears in visitors' browsers.

To find these options, go to your [Dashboard > Customize](https://miwa.lol/dashboard/customize), then go to the *SEO & Metadata* tab. A live preview of the tab is shown next to the options.

## Options

### Tab icon

Chooses the icon shown in the browser tab:

- **Miwa.lol logo** - the default.
- **Your avatar** - uses your profile avatar.
- **Custom favicon** - upload your own icon.

#### Custom favicon

Selecting *Custom favicon* reveals an upload field. The file must be **256KB or smaller**, and can be a `.png`, `.jpg`, `.ico`, `.svg`, or `.webp`.

<Aside type="info">
  Switching the tab icon back to *Miwa.lol logo* or *Your avatar* removes the uploaded favicon.
</Aside>

### Animated title

If enabled, your page title (`@username`) will be animated in the tab. You can choose from several animations for your page title in the tab:
* **None**: No animation.
* **Typewriter**: The title will appear as if typed by a typewriter.
* **Reverse**: The title will alternate between `@username` and `@emanresu`.
* **Left/Right**: The title will move back and forth horizontally.
