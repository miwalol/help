**When someone shares your profile link on Discord, X, Instagram, or anywhere else that unfurls links, a preview card is generated.** The embed settings let you decide what that card says and how it looks.

To find them, go to your [Dashboard > Customize](https://miwa.lol/dashboard/customize), then go to the *SEO & Metadata* tab and scroll down to the *Embed* section.

By default the embed is off, and your profile falls back to a generic preview. Click **Enable** to turn it on and reveal the options below. A live preview, styled like a Discord embed, updates as you type.

## Options

### Title

The bold heading of the embed. Left empty, it falls back to `@yourusername` (or your display name if you set one).

### Author

A small line of text shown above the title.

### Description

The text below the title. Left empty, it falls back to your first typewriter text if you use one, and to your [description](/customize/profile-card/#description) otherwise.

### Color

The colored bar down the left side of the embed. Tick **Use profile color** to keep it in sync with your [accent color](/customize/colors/#accent-color) instead of picking one here.

### Background image

The large image shown inside the embed. The recommended size is **1200x630px**.

Tick **Use profile background** to reuse your [profile background](/customize/assets/#background) instead of uploading a separate image.

<Aside type="tip">
  Not every platform shows every field. Discord renders all of them; others may only show the title, description, and image.
</Aside>
