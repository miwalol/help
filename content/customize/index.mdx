**Welcome to the customization overview!** This section provides a comprehensive guide on how to personalize your Miwa.lol profile to make it uniquely yours.

All customization options live under your [Dashboard > Customize](https://miwa.lol/dashboard/customize), organized across five tabs:

| Tab                 | What you'll find there                                                              |
|---------------------|-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **General**         | Your description, the profile card (opacity, blur, font, layout, border…), and more |
| **Assets**          | Avatar, background, custom cursor, and audios                                        |
| **Colors & Effects**| Colors, glow, cursor and background effects, opening animation                       |
| **SEO & Metadata**  | Browser tab appearance, and how your page looks when shared                          |
| **Privacy**         | Hide your views, likes, join date, and search engine indexing                        |
