**To create a template, go to your Dashboard > [Templates](https://miwa.lol/dashboard/templates),** then click the *Create Template* button.

A template is a snapshot of your current customization, so set your profile up the way you want it before creating one.

## Options

- **Preview:** A preview of the template. We recommend taking a screenshot of your page.
- **Name:** The template name. Can be anything you want.
- **Tags:** Labels describing your template. At least one is required. They're shown on your template, and clicking one searches the gallery for that word.
- **Visibility:** Controls who can find and use your template:
  - **Public:** Listed and searchable by anyone in the public templates list.
  - **Unlisted:** Hidden from the list and search, but anyone with the link can use it.
  - **Private:** Only you can see and use it.

## After creating

Your template appears in the **My templates** tab. From there you can edit its name, tags, and visibility at any time, copy its link, or delete it.

<Aside type="info">
  The preview image can't be changed after the template is created. If you want a different one, delete the template and create it again.
</Aside>
