**Templates are user-created pre-designed "profiles" that you can apply to yours to quickly change its appearance.**

To browse them, navigate to the *Templates* section in the dashboard sidebar.

## Browsing templates

The Templates section has three tabs:

- **Templates** - the public gallery, where you can find everyone's templates.
- **My templates** - the ones you created, whatever their visibility.
- **Liked** - the ones you liked, so you can find them again later.

### Searching and sorting

Use the search field to look for a template by its **name** or by its **creator's username**. Clicking a tag on a template runs a search for that word too.

The dropdown next to it sorts the gallery by:

- **Newest** (the default)
- **Oldest**
- **Trending**
- **Most liked**

## Previewing and applying

Click <Eye /> **Preview** on a template to see it applied to your own profile, without saving anything. Once you're happy with it, click **Apply**.

<Aside type="warning">
  Applying a template will override your current customization settings, including the layout, colors, fonts, and other visual aspects of your profile.
</Aside>

Each template also shows how many times it has been used, and who made it.

## Liking a template

Click the <Heart /> icon on a template to like it. Liked templates are collected in the **Liked** tab, and likes feed the *Most liked* sorting so good templates surface for everyone.

## Free vs Premium Templates

**Some templates are available for free, while others are exclusive to Premium users.** What makes a template Premium is the options used in it. If a template uses any Premium-only options, it will be marked as Premium.

## Managing your own templates

On your own templates you'll also find:

- <Pencil /> **Edit** - change the name, tags, and visibility. The preview image can't be changed after creation.
- <LinkIcon /> **Copy link** - handy for sharing an *Unlisted* template.
- <Trash2 /> **Delete** - removes the template for good.
