**By adding links to your profile, you can make your profile more interactive and allow visitors to easily access your other social media accounts, websites, or any other links you want to share.**

<Aside type="info">
  **Free accounts can have up to 5 links.** [Premium accounts](https://miwa.lol/pricing) have no limit.
</Aside>

## Editing Links

To change a link, click the <Pencil /> icon next to it. This reopens the same editor you used to add it, so you can correct the value, switch between *Link* and *Copyable Text*, or replace the icon of a custom link.

## Hiding Links

**You don't have to delete a link to take it off your profile.** Click the <Eye /> icon next to a link to hide it: it stays in your dashboard, shown greyed out with an <EyeOff /> icon, but visitors won't see it. Click the icon again to bring it back.

<Aside type="tip">
  This is handy for links you only want up some of the time, or for a platform you're taking a break from.
</Aside>

## Reordering Links

To reorder your links, simply drag and drop them in the desired order. The order you set here will be reflected on your profile.

## Removing Links

To remove a link, click the <Trash2 /> icon next to the link you want to delete.
